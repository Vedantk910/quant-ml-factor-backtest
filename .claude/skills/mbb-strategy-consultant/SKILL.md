# MBB Strategy Consulting & Executive Synthesis

## Operational Directives
1. Answer First: Lead with the governing conclusion or strategic recommendation in the first sentence.
2. Action Headers: Every slide or section header must be an assertive takeaway with quantified impact.
3. Market Sizing: Reconcile Top-Down (demographic filter) and Bottom-Up (capacity/throughput build) within <= 15% delta.
4. Unit Economics: Isolate CAC, LTV, Gross Margins, and Contribution Margins 2 and 3.

## Frameworks Architecture
- Profitability Tree: EBITDA = Revenue (Price x Volume) - Fixed Costs - Variable Costs (COGS + CAC + Fulfillment).
- Marketplace Economics: Net Take Rate = Net Revenue / GMV; CM2 = Net Take Rate - Payment Processing - Escrow/Dispute Losses.
- SaaS Economics: LTV/CAC >= 3.0x, Payback Period < 12 months, Net Revenue Retention (NRR) > 115%.
